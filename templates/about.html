About MEDExplain AI

MEDExplain AI is an educational healthcare information tool designed to help patients understand complex medical laboratory reports in clear, compassionate, doctor-style language.

Our Mission

Medical test reports are often filled with clinical acronyms, technical measurements, and complex reference values that can leave patients feeling anxious or confused. MEDExplain AI translates lab parameters into plain language and highlights key test ranges to empower patients during conversations with their healthcare providers.

Educational Safety & Non-Diagnostic Commitment

1

No Automated Diagnosis

MEDExplain AI does not diagnose illnesses, diseases, or medical conditions under any circumstance.

2

No Treatment Prescriptions

We do not prescribe medications, dosage adjustments, or clinical treatments.

3

Empowering Doctor Dialogue

We provide curated questions to help patients have informed, constructive discussions with their licensed physicians.

Supported Multilingual Explanations

To ensure accessibility, MEDExplain AI supports explanations in English and regional Indian languages (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi) while preserving exact medical terminology definitions and test values.

Educational Disclaimer: Always seek the advice of your physician or qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or test result.
